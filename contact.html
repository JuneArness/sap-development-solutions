Questions

Good to know.

How does a project start?

Send the form or call. We’ll talk through your goals, then send a clear plan and estimate before any work begins.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Websites are delivered as standard files that belong to you. Static sites can be hosted on GitHub Pages under your own account (free for public repositories), or anywhere you choose.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. Every website handoff includes step-by-step guides for common updates, and help is available if you need it.

Is business formation support legal advice?

No. We help with the practical, organizational and digital side of getting started. SAP Development Solutions is not a law firm or accounting firm, so please consult a licensed professional for legal, tax or accounting advice.
